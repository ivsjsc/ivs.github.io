Common questions

Foreign Teacher FAQ

Can I teach in Vietnam just because I entered on a valid visa?

No assumption should be made from entry status alone. Lawful entry/residence and lawful employment are separate compliance questions. Your employer should identify the applicable work-authorization basis before you teach.

Where should I apply for a Vietnam e‑Visa?

Start from the Vietnam Immigration Department or an official government public-service portal. Avoid sponsored search ads or unofficial domains. Check the live official procedure for eligibility, fee, validity and processing information.

Does every foreign teacher need a work permit?

Foreign-worker law contains different legal categories, including possible exemptions, but exemption does not always mean there is no administrative obligation. The exact current category and filing requirements must be checked for your case.

What documents should I prepare as a foreign teacher?

A practical readiness pack usually includes passport/immigration records, CV, degree, teaching/professional qualifications and experience evidence. Health, criminal-record, legalization and translation documents may also be required depending on the exact legal procedure.

Can I post my passport or work documents in a Facebook/Zalo group?

Do not publish sensitive identity, criminal-record, banking, residence or health documents in public groups. Use community channels for peer discussion and a secure verified channel for formal document exchange.

Where can I find teaching jobs?

Use Aivy TeacherMatch at ivslearning.top for the dedicated IVS recruitment workflow. The IVS Global Teacher Hub focuses on knowledge, preparation, legal/admin navigation and support.

How do I check whether an old legal guide is still valid?

Search the legal document number on the National Legal Database or Government Legal Documents portal. Check current effect, amendments, replacement instruments and the date the administrative procedure was last updated.

Does IVS provide legal advice?

This Hub provides general educational and practical information. Legal requirements can depend on nationality, role, employer type, location and current law. For a legal decision or dispute, confirm with the competent authority or a qualified professional.
