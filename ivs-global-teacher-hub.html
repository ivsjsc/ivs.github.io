Legal readiness

A safer order for foreign-teacher compliance

Immigration, employment and education requirements are connected but distinct compliance layers.

1

Lawful entry

Know your passport, entry basis and current immigration status.

2

Residence

Confirm temporary residence and, where eligible, longer-term residence status.

3

Work authorization

Identify the correct permit or exemption basis before teaching.

4

Teacher qualification

Check education-sector requirements for the exact role/provider.

5

Tax & insurance

Set up payroll, tax and applicable statutory insurance correctly.
